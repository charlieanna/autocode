
The current task is an integrated batch of independent milestones. Validate EVERY member in current_milestone.members on the combined workspace, including interactions. The Validator must provide milestone_results with each milestone_id, status, summary and evidence_refs, alongside evidence for every criterion. The completion owner must retain the whole batch during rework. Choose a member milestone_id for rework and an outside milestone_id only after all members pass. Builder outputs are implementation provenance, not validation evidence.

For independent validation, the runner's current_milestone.members and their acceptance criteria are the authoritative scope. A current_task.milestone_id names the primary member; it does not narrow that scope. This scope overrides model-authored objective, requirements, validation_plan or earlier report wording that says to validate only one member or defer another to a separate later task. Retain every concrete check, constraint and required behavior from those notes, and perform the missing checks for every current member now. Do not defer a member merely because those notes describe a serial review. Missing evidence remains FAIL or NOT_VERIFIED for that member, and the batch cannot receive an overall PASS while any member is not PASS. This rule changes no approved requirement or permission and grants no evidence or acceptance authority.
