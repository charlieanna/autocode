Add two commands to stock.py. `stock.py move SKU QTY FROM TO` moves QTY units of SKU from location FROM to location TO. `stock.py remove SKU QTY LOCATION` removes QTY units of SKU at LOCATION; a holding that reaches 0 is no longer listed by `show`. Both follow the exit-code contract in README.md. They refuse a QTY that is not a positive integer, a FROM equal to TO, taking more units of a SKU than its location holds (including a SKU or location that holds none), and a malformed stock.json. Add tests to tests/test_stock.py for both commands and for each refusal, and document the commands in README.md. Python standard library only.
