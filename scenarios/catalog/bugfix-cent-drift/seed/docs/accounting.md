# Accounting rules

1. Every amount we invoice, charge or refund is a whole number of cents, rounded
   **half up** (0.005 becomes 0.01).
2. A line's amount is price × quantity less its discount, rounded to the cent.
3. Sales tax is 8.25%. Whether tax is rounded per line or once on the invoice
   subtotal has not been settled with our accountant; either is acceptable as
   long as the invoice shows each line's share of the tax.
4. The amount charged to the card is exactly the invoice total.
5. Refunding every line of an order refunds exactly what was charged, never a
   cent more or less. A line's refund is its amount plus its share of the tax.
